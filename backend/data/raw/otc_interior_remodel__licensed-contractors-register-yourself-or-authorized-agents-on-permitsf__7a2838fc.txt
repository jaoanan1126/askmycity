Authorized agents should add you as a "guest" to any permits under your license.For streamlined serviceAuthorized agents connected to an AUTH record will no longer need to provide authorized agent forms nor driver’s licenses every time when applying for a permit.For eligible reroofing projects, applicants connected to an AUTH record can get their permit right away.What to knowRequirementsYou must be the contractor license holder to use this form.Any authorized agents you add will be connected to your license when they apply for building permits. Have your authorized agents create a free account in PermitSFEach of your authorized agents should sign up at PermitSF.Note the email addresses they used to create a PermitSF account. You will need to enter it in the form.2. Gather information about your contractor license and authorized agentsWe will ask you for:Your business name or California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) numberYour San Francisco Business Account Number (BAN)A JPEG image of a government-issued ID, like your Driver’s License, state-issued ID card, alien registration card, passport, or form notarizationFull names and email addresses used for PermitSF accounts, for each authorized agent you want to add3. Complete the contractor registration formFill out the form online at PermitSF.Apply onlineAfter you applyAfter you submit the form, the application will be identified by a record number starting with "AUTH."We will review your application, verify your license and whether you're listed on your CSLB personnel list, and connect any authorized agents to your license in PermitSF within 2 business days.You or your agents may be asked for the AUTH record number when applying for permits, to help streamline approvals.Special casesAdding or removing authorized agents after initial approvalFollow these steps to edit your contractor authorization record:Sign into your PermitSF profile.Once logged in, go to your "Dashboard" on the left side of the screen.Under "Action Required," locate "Renew your Contractor Authorization" and select "Renew Now."Change in license ownershipIf you sell your contracting business, have the new contractor license holder submit a new contractor authorization form.
