You can upload the associated electrical permit after you submit the fire alarm permit application.What to do1. Gather information about your fire alarm systemWe will ask you for:Information about the building, including type of construction, existing use, and occupancy classificationNumber of devices used (initiating, notification, and control units or power supplies)Fire alarm plans, as an upload (with file names only in alphanumeric characters, hyphens, and underscores)Associated building permit numbersIf your work is for a new install, we will also ask for information about:Coverage of the alarm system, in square feetFire Alarm Control Units (FACU)Remote annunciator panelsNumber of fixtures (detectors, strobes, speakers, etc)Reference architectural plans, with file names only in alphanumeric characters, hyphens, and underscores (optional)Refer to SF Fire Administrative Bulletin 2.01 for more details about fire alarm and signaling system submittals.2. Apply for a fire alarm permit onlineLicensed contractors can use PermitSF to apply online. The application will ask you about your planned fire alarm work. We will review your application and send you the fee amount.Work considered over-the-counter in the previous process (express) will be approved in about two business days. Work considered in-house in the previous process (standard) will be reviewed in 30 days.Apply online3. Apply for an electrical permit onlineYou can apply for an electrical permit at the same time as your fire alarm permit, or later.You must upload a copy of your electrical permit to the fire alarm permit before you can schedule an inspection. File names should only in alphanumeric characters, hyphens, and underscores.We will do a combined fire alarm and electrical inspection.Print out your permit and approved plan sets for the inspection.Special casesFire sprinkler monitoringYou will need a fire alarm permit when you use a fire alarm panel to monitor fire sprinkler or suppression systems. We will ask for your sprinkler plans in the fire alarm permit.As-build permitsIf you are submitting a revision to a previous permit, apply for your revision permit online for an online permit. If the previous permit was submitted on paper, the revision permit also needs to be submitted on paper.RelatedApply for an electrical permitBuilding permitsGet a fire sprinkler permitPartner agenciesFire Department
