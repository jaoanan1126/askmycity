Info PageWhere to apply for your permitSan Francisco offers multiple ways to apply for a permit based on your project.Department of Building InspectionStart by identifying the permit type your project requires. Then choose the process that applies to your project: PermitSF Portal, Contractor Permits, Over-the-Counter or In-House Review.Apply in the PermitSF PortalApply in the PermitSF portal for the following projects:Reroofing without plansSigns and awnings (new install and legalization)Door replacement (in-kind)Window or skylight replacement (in-kind)Exterior siding repair or replacement (in-kind)Fire alarmsFire sprinklersWater flow information requestsYou will first need to create a free account on OpenGov, then apply through the PermitSF Portal.More permit types will be added to the PermitSF Portal in the coming months.Apply for Contractor Permits onlineApply online for permits that are issued immediately and do not require plans. You must be a registered contractor to use this option.Apply for a Contractor Permit online for projects such as:Electrical permitPlumbing and mechanical permitsSolar photovoltaic systemKitchen or bath remodelPermit to Operate Boiler (PTO)Apply for Over-the-Counter permits at the Permit CenterApply in person at the Permit Center for projects that can be reviewed the same day or within a short timeframe. Over-the-Counter permits may require plans depending on the scope of work.Over-the-Counter permits without plans include, but are not limited to:In-kind repair decks and/or stairs (less than 50%)In-kind minor dry rot repairIn-kind kitchen remodel (no changes to the floor plan or walls)In-kind bathroom remodel (no change to the floor plan or walls)In-kind replacement of garage doors (same size and same location)Exploratory work to determine depth of footing, stud size/spacing, floor/roof joist size and direction.
