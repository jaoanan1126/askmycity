Step-by-stepGet an Over-the-Counter permit for your interior residential remodelFollow these instructions for projects like kitchen, bath, and other interior residential remodels.Department of Building InspectionThe Department of Building Inspection reviews every building permit application for life safety and building code compliance.Most interior residential remodels can be reviewed Over-the-Counter (OTC). Review our instructions to prepare the right forms and book a time to submit your building permit application.If you are a registered contractor, you can apply for kitchen and bath remodels online.If your interior residential project is not eligible for OTC review, follow the steps for in-house review.11Check where to apply for your permitWays to apply for a permitThe City offers multiple ways to apply for a permit, including the PermitSF Portal, Online Contractor Permits, Over-the-Counter, and In-House Review.See a list of which permit types are available for each way to applyIdentify your permit type and how to applyChoose the option below that applies to your project.If your permit type is available in the PermitSF Portal, create a free OpenGov account to apply for a permit and track its status.If your permit type is available as an Online Contractor Permit, apply through our online system.If your permit type is available Over-the-Counter, apply in person at the Permit Center.If your permit requires In-House Review, submit your application for plan review.Licensed contractorsFor PermitSF Portal applications, verify your license and add any authorized agents to your license before applying. Learn how to register yourself or authorized agents in the PermitSF Portal.For Online Contractor Permits, Over-the-Counter, or In-House Review, register with the City as a licensed contractor before
