Check if your boiler is required to have a PTOThere must be an active PTO on file for a six unit or more with one of the following:More than 200,000 total BTU capacity for boiler or tankless water heaters (single or combined)More than 120 gallons of storage tank capacity (single or combined)If combined units are used in series, each unit will need a separate PTO application.You do not need a PTO to replace a boiler with a heat pump system because it does not change how the building is used or occupied. Heat pumps are more energy efficient than boilers.For more information and to see if your property or building meets our requirements, you may view our Permit to Operate Boiler Criteria Checklist.2.
